## 2. Ready-to-Post Copy (Josh's Main Page, Reels Captions & Regional Groups)

### Post A: The All-in-One Launch Post (Why we built it)
> Doing deliveries and talking to farmers every week, we get asked the same things over and over:
> "Do you know an MC driver or header operator available right now?"
> "Who's got lucerne or wheaten hay around Central West?"
> "Who can put up 5km of exclusion fence?"
>
> Facebook groups are great for a yarn, but a mess when you actually need to search by postcode, ticket, or commodity — posts disappear in 24 hours.
>
> So we built **Outback Connections** — a free, searchable rural work board and marketplace:
> - **Work & Skills Board:** Drivers (MC/HC/HR), header & tractor operators, fencers, shearers and station hands can list their tickets, rates & travel radius, or properties can post jobs.
> - **Livestock, Hay, Grain & Gear:** List what you have **For Sale** or post **Wanted to Buy** so sellers come to you. 100% free, 0% commission.
> - **Freight & Backloads:** Match empty truck deck space with loads going your way.
> - **Rural Services Directory:** Search local fencing contractors, earthmovers & rural services by postcode.
>
> Jump on and put your listing up in 2 minutes (free):
> https://www.outbackconnections.com.au/?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=josh_organic&utm_campaign=rural_hub

## 4. What to Watch After Each Post
- `/dashboard/admin/analytics` → **Traction gate & Where people came from:** tracks verified human searches, enquiries, claims, and UTM campaign breakdown (`rural_hub`, `work_board`, `livestock_harvest`, `freight_backloads`, `fencing_contractors`).
- `/dashboard/admin/demand` → **Forecasting & Market Intelligence:** tracks monthly & regional commodity/livestock supply (`For Sale`) vs demand (`Wanted to Buy`), cleared sold prices & volumes, worker availability vs hiring demand by ticket, and **Live search signals** (including zero-result searches where farmers are searching for a ticket or commodity that needs more supply). All tables have 1-click CSV export.
